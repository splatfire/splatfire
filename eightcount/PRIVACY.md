# EightCount – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 October 2026_

**Short version: EightCount collects no data about you. Everything stays on
your iPhone.** There are no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no
tracking. The app does not connect to the internet.

## What the app stores, and where

All of this is stored **only on your device**, inside the app:

- the dance videos you import (a copy, so they stay if you delete them from Photos),
- your notes, sections, progress marks, formations and pose snapshots,
- the videos you record of yourself, and their AI analysis,
- your settings and the days you practised (for the streak).

Deleting a project deletes its data. Deleting the app deletes everything.
The developer cannot see any of it.

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Camera** – to record you dancing along with the tutorial. Recordings stay
  on your iPhone.
- **Photos** – to import a dance video you pick, and to save a video to your
  library when you tap "Save to Photos".
- **Notifications** (only if you turn on review reminders) – local reminders
  created on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server.

## Features that use Apple technology on your device

- **AI dance analysis and tips** use Apple's Vision framework and Apple's
  on-device language model. They run on your iPhone; your videos are not
  uploaded.
- **Spoken counts** use the iPhone's built-in voice (on the device).
- **Apple Watch remote** sends play/pause and which 8-count is playing
  between your iPhone and your own Apple Watch.
- **AirPlay** sends the video to a TV you choose.

## Sharing

The app only shares something when you ask it to: for example when you
export a project file, save a video to Photos, or use the share sheet. Where
it goes is up to you.

## Children

EightCount does not collect personal information from anyone, including
children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
